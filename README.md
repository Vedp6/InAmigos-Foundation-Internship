* 📱 Mobile-first donations
* 💳 Online giving
* 🔄 Recurring donations
* 🌐 Crowdfunding
* 🤖 AI in fundraising
* 🔎 Donor engagement

* 🇮🇳 Digital fundraising in India

The research highlighted the importance of:

* Frictionless digital donation experiences
* Mobile-optimized fundraising
* Recurring giving programs

* Transparent impact reporting

The research was supported using AI-assisted web research and data synthesis from nonprofit-sector sources.

# 🎨 Task 5 — NGO UI/UX Design

Designed and implemented a complete NGO website layout based on a Figma design concept.

### Design Concept

**Roots & Reach**

The design focuses on creating a trustworthy, modern, and community-oriented NGO experience.

### Main Sections

* Navigation
* Hero section
* Donation section
* Volunteer registration
* Impact showcase
* Testimonials
* Campaigns & events
* Contact & support
* Footer

### Donation Experience

The design includes:

* One-time donation
* Monthly donation
* Preset donation amounts
* Custom amount
* Donor information
* Donation CTA

### Volunteer Experience

A four-step volunteer flow was designed:

```text
01 → Tell us your skills & availability
02 → Match with a nearby project
03 → Attend orientation
04 → Start volunteering
```

### Impact Showcase

The design communicates measurable impact through statistics such as:

```text
128       Wells built
6,400     Children in school programs
340       Farmers trained
86%       Funds directly to programs
```

### Events & Campaigns

The interface also includes upcoming:

* Fundraisers
* On-ground activities
* Community events
