About Us

Built by volunteers, run for communities.

InAmigos Foundation began in 2019 with a handful of college volunteers distributing books in a village outside Bilaspur. Today we run six programs across ten states — but the model hasn't changed: show up, listen first, and build what the community actually asks for.

We're a registered Section 8 non-profit, which means every rupee we raise is accounted for and every partnership goes through proper compliance — so donors and partners can trust exactly where support lands.

What We Do

Six programs, one goal.

Every initiative is designed with the community it serves, not for it — from classrooms to kitchens to forest patches.

Shiksha

Education

After-school learning centres, study material, and mentorship for children who'd otherwise have neither the books nor the time.

Udaan

Women Empowerment

Vocational training, financial literacy, and small-business support so women build income on their own terms.

Prakriti

Environment

Tree plantation drives, plastic-free market campaigns, and neighbourhood clean-ups run by local volunteer chapters.

Sewa

Animal Welfare & Health

Free vaccination camps, rescue support, and basic health check-up drives in underserved neighbourhoods.

Anna

Food Security

Weekly ration kits and hot-meal drives for families going through an unexpected income shock.

Kaushal

Skill Development

Digital literacy and job-readiness workshops for youth transitioning out of school into work.

Featured Program

Project Udaan — teaching flight, not dependence.

In its first two years, Project Udaan has trained over 800 women across three districts in tailoring, food processing, and basic bookkeeping — skills that turn into steady weekly income within months, not years.

"I used to wait for my husband's wage on Fridays. Now I have my own account, and my own Fridays."

We plan to bring Udaan to seven more districts by 2027, working with local self-help groups already trusted by the women we hope to reach.
